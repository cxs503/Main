# Industry-learning-driven autonomous construction

Status: proposed research method, 2026-10-25. No controlled cross-industry result is claimed.

## Objective

Investigate whether an explicit, evidence-checked industry knowledge structure improves agents' ability to build verifiable industrial digital systems with less external assistance. Target workflow:

Industry tutorial → process/data/constraint model → components and dependencies → integrated application → autonomous development → evidence-driven revision of knowledge and implementation.

The tutorial is a versioned engineering artifact, not an assumed source of truth. Start with a landscape outline and one runnable slice; do not wait for an exhaustive textbook before implementing. Separate sourced facts, modelling assumptions and unknown customer requirements. Link important claims to process definitions, source components, scenarios and acceptance evidence. Implementation failures can trigger tutorial revision, preserving both the original claim and its correction.

## Three layers

1. Organization: durable identity, coordination, evidence handoff, independent verification and recovery.
2. Industrial methods: units, data quality, balances, time series, simulation, prediction, optimization, reporting and rollback.
3. Industry adaptation: equipment/process knowledge, parameters, constraints, data connections and acceptance definitions.

Reuse must be measured through actual integration and verification. Renaming the same equipment demonstration is not evidence of industry transfer. Distinguish outline coverage, verified knowledge, implemented source, actual execution, independent replay, application integration and real-data validation.

## Proposed evaluation

With organization configuration initially held fixed, compare:

- Direct task execution.
- Retrieval of the same available industry material.
- A generated and frozen tutorial used to guide construction.
- A tutorial/process/component structure with evidence-driven revision.

Count tutorial generation, source verification and maintenance costs. Use frozen initial code, inputs, tools, permissions and independent acceptance. Evaluate end-to-end acceptance, knowledge omissions/errors, total resources, time, external intervention and industry-specific adaptation effort. Unknown costs remain missing rather than retrospectively invented. Numerical truth, boundary constraints, hidden tests and held-out time periods are primary evidence; model agreement is supplementary.

A steel-energy application can serve as a development slice, followed by non-energy steel processes and an adaptation industry such as rare earths. Once an industry is used for tuning, it is not an unseen confirmation domain. Choose genuinely held-out tasks/domains separately. Synthetic results do not establish physical fidelity, field savings or production-control safety.

## Recursive improvement and research leadership

An external research lead designs experiments, audits evidence and can modify the platform. Agents are both research subjects and participants: they can investigate and modify their own harness, tools and working methods. Attribute changes as externally initiated, autonomously initiated or mixed. Record initiator, implementer, prompts/interventions, exact source and loaded versions, exposure time, resource cost, regression evidence, activation and rollback.

Use separate online exploration and frozen evaluation environments. Conditions permitting self-modification must declare the permitted scope and preserve immutable assessment data. Agents cannot rewrite hidden truth or prior failures to improve their score. The implementer cannot be the sole independent verifier; the research lead's changes require independent verification too. Protocol implementation, self-reported closure and actual industrial effectiveness remain separate claims.

## Current implementation boundary

This document defines a research agenda. The accompanying workspace reliability patch supports preservation of development work and accurate source/check receipts. It does not implement a complete industry adapter, claim autonomous cross-industry construction, or establish an organizational causal effect. Future work should implement provenance links, intervention/resource measurement and small controlled pilots before expanding experiment scale.
